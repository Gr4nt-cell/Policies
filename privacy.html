Privacy Policy — iDialer (Phone) (com.gr4nt.dialer)

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · app version 1.0.18

iDialer (shown as Phone inside the app) is a phone and contacts app for Android, made by Gr4nt. This policy explains what the app accesses, what it does with it, and what it never does.

The short version: Phone shows ads from Google AdMob, which you can remove with a one-time purchase. It has no analytics or tracking of its own. Your calls, call history and contacts are processed on your device and are never sent to us or to the ad network. Optional Google Contacts backup uses Android account sync, and actions you choose may pass information to another app as described below.

1. What the app accesses, and why

The app only asks for what a phone app needs. Each permission is used only for the purpose listed here, and only on your device.

AccessWhy the app needs it
Phone calls (make and manage calls, phone state, default phone app)To place, answer, hold, merge and end calls, choose a SIM, run USSD codes, and show the call screen.
Call logTo show your recent calls, call statistics and frequently called contacts, and to remove calls you delete.
ContactsTo show your contacts, put names and photos on calls, and add or edit contacts when you ask.
Camera and photosOnly when you choose to take or pick a contact photo.
NotificationsTo show incoming and ongoing calls, with answer, decline and hang-up buttons.
Vibration and wake lockTo vibrate for incoming calls, and to turn the screen off when the phone is at your ear.

2. What is stored, and where

  • Settings, favourites and blocked numbers are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Contacts and call history stay in Android's own contacts and call-log databases. The app reads them and changes them only when you ask (for example adding a contact or deleting calls).
  • Contact photos are read from the phone's contacts and displayed on the call screen when enabled. When Depth Effect is on, Google Play services provides an on-device subject-segmentation model; the photo is processed on your phone and is not uploaded. Google Play services may download that model when needed.
  • Contacts backup to Google Contacts is off until you turn it on in Settings › Contacts Backup. When it's on, new contacts are saved to the Google account you choose on your phone, and contacts stored only on the phone are moved into it. The upload is done by Android's own Google account sync, under Google's privacy policy; the app itself uses the internet only for ads and sends nothing to us. Turn it off at any time; contacts already in your Google account stay there and can be managed at https://contacts.google.com.

The app is excluded from Android cloud backups, so your settings aren't copied to Google Drive or any other backup service.

3. Ads, purchases and sharing

  • Phone uses the internet only to show ads. Ads come from Google AdMob, which receives your device's advertising ID, IP address and basic device information, and may use them to choose and measure ads, under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/technologies/ads).
  • In the EU, the UK and similar regions you're asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Your calls, call history, contacts and photos are never shared with the ad network or with us.
  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google only tells the app whether the purchase was made, so ads stay off on your Google account.
  • There are no analytics or crash-reporting libraries, and no data is sold.
  • Some actions hand you over to another app you choose, such as WhatsApp, Messages, your notes app or the share sheet. The Notes button on the call screen passes the caller's name or number and the time to the notes app you picked, only when you tap it. What happens next is covered by that app's privacy policy.
  • Calls themselves are carried by your mobile network, as with any phone app.

4. Your controls

In Settings › Privacy you can:

  • hide who is calling on the lock screen;
  • block screenshots and app previews of the app.

You can also delete calls from Recents, and remove any permission in Android's settings. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

5. Children

The app isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new "Last updated" date. The app will never start collecting or sharing data without updating this policy first.
