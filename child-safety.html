About iDialer

iDialer (Phone) is an Android dialer and contacts app developed by Gr4nt. Calls are carried by the user’s mobile network. The app has no internet permission and does not provide public profiles, anonymous or random chat, a social feed, media sharing, or a service for hosting or distributing user content.

Contacts and call history are handled on the device. These standards prohibit misuse of the app even though it does not operate a content-sharing platform.

Our standards against CSAE

Using iDialer to facilitate or encourage any child sexual abuse or exploitation is prohibited. This includes:

  • Creating, obtaining, possessing, sharing, promoting or distributing CSAM, including computer-generated material depicting child sexual abuse.
  • Sexual grooming, solicitation or sexual communication intended to exploit a child.
  • Sexual extortion, coercion, trafficking or any other sexual exploitation of a child.
  • Sexualising children or arranging, encouraging or facilitating conduct that sexually abuses or endangers them.

For these standards, a child is a person under 18 years old. These prohibitions apply to all users.

Report a child-safety concern

You can contact our designated child-safety contact at gstephen116@gmail.com.

In the app, open Settings → Safety → Report a Child Safety Concern. The reporting options include contacting the developer by email and opening the NCMEC CyberTipline to report suspected child sexual exploitation.

Describe the concern and how it relates to iDialer. Include only information necessary for us to understand and respond. Do not attach or forward CSAM or other illegal material.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services or police immediately.

This email address is not an emergency service.

How we respond to reports

Reports of suspected CSAE or CSAM connected to iDialer are treated as a priority. We review the available information and take appropriate action within our control, consistent with these standards and applicable law.

iDialer does not host a shared content service. If CSAM is identified in content under our control, we remove or disable access to it and take appropriate steps required by applicable law, including any required preservation and reporting.

We report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional or national authority as required by applicable law. Information about a child at risk is referred to appropriate authorities when required. We cooperate with lawful requests made through proper legal channels.

Child-safety responsibilities

We are committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and to acting on reports in accordance with these published standards. Our designated contact can receive child-safety notifications, discuss the app’s CSAM prevention and response practices, and coordinate appropriate action.
